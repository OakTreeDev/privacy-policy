LUSA Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026
Last Updated: September 27, 2026

LUSA ("LUSA", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a productivity and personal development application designed to help users manage goals, tasks, progress, and other personal activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how LUSA handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

LUSA v0.1 does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or location.

Information entered into LUSA, such as tasks, goals, progress, experience points, levels, streaks, statistics, and preferences, is stored locally on the user's device and is used to provide the App's features.

LUSA v0.1 does not transmit this information to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

Information stored by LUSA is used solely to provide and operate the App's features, including:

- Saving tasks and goals
- Tracking progress
- Calculating experience points and levels
- Maintaining streaks
- Displaying statistics
- Remembering user preferences

LUSA does not sell, rent, or otherwise share users' personal information with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Backup

LUSA v0.1 stores applicable data locally on the user's device.

LUSA v0.1 does not provide cloud synchronization or cloud backup.

Deleting the App, clearing its application data, or losing or resetting the device may result in the loss of locally stored LUSA data.

Users are responsible for maintaining any backups they consider necessary.

4. Third-Party Services

LUSA v0.1 does not currently use third-party services for advertising, analytics, user tracking, account management, or cloud synchronization.

The App may use software libraries and development tools necessary for its operation.

If future versions of LUSA introduce third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

5. Device Permissions

LUSA is designed to use application-specific storage for locally stored information and does not currently require broad device storage permissions for its normal operation.

Future versions may require additional permissions if new features are introduced. Any such changes will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy where applicable.

6. Children's Privacy

LUSA does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its current features.

Because LUSA v0.1 does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information, the App does not currently operate a system for collecting users' personal information.

7. Data Security

LUSA v0.1 does not transmit users' locally stored information to LUSA-operated servers.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices and protecting access to locally stored information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

LUSA's features and data practices may change over time.

We may therefore update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the App, its features, applicable laws, or the way information is handled.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last Updated" date will be changed accordingly.

If significant changes are made to how user information is collected, stored, or processed, additional notice may be provided where appropriate.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically to remain informed about LUSA's privacy practices.

9. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or LUSA's privacy practices, you may contact us at:

Email: lusa.support@gmail.com

10. Current Version

This Privacy Policy describes the data practices of LUSA v0.1 as of the date stated above.

Future versions of LUSA may introduce additional features or services that change these practices. This Privacy Policy will be revised accordingly.
